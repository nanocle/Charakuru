## How to assess revenue

- **Revenue included**: Count gross revenue across the individual, business, or organization's entire business, not just Kit-based products or activities. Use the amount before expenses and contractor costs, excluding consumption or sales taxes collected for tax authorities. For an independent individual, unrelated employment salary and personal transfers are not business revenue.
- **Assessment period**: Use the most recently completed fiscal year. An independent individual uses the most recently completed calendar year (January 1–December 31) unless the business uses another regular fiscal year. Do not choose a convenient period solely for this License.
- **No defined fiscal year**: Use the immediately preceding twelve months at the assessment date.
- **New businesses**: If no fiscal year has yet ended, use actual revenue since the business began, limited to the immediately preceding twelve months if the business has operated longer. Do not annualize revenue for a business operating for less than twelve months or for a short initial fiscal year.
- **Use for an organization**: Assess the business or organization on whose behalf the Kit is used, not just an employee or contractor's personal income. Assess commonly controlled entities as a group, excluding duplicate internal transfers. If their fiscal year-end dates differ, use each entity's most recently completed fiscal year and reassess whenever any such year ends.

## How to assess funding

- **Cumulative amount**: Count external equity investment and business-purpose loan proceeds actually received since the business began, including convertible financing. This is not limited to the most recent fiscal year or twelve months. Spending or repaying funds does not reduce the cumulative amount.
- **Excluded amounts**: Exclude revenue, a founder's unborrowed personal funds contributed to a business independently owned and controlled by that founder, personal borrowing unrelated to the business, and non-repayable grants or gifts. Do not count refinancing of the same outstanding loan principal twice; count only additional proceeds.
- **Controlled groups**: As with revenue, assess the commonly controlled group as a whole and exclude duplicate transfers within the group.

## Reassessment and agreement timing

Check eligibility before starting business use and at each fiscal year-end. The new revenue assessment applies from the start of the next fiscal year; tax filing or formal approval of accounts does not postpone it. Current-year revenue alone does not change the revenue assessment during that fiscal year. If there is no defined or completed fiscal year, continue to monitor actual revenue using the periods above.

Reassess funding when additional qualifying funds are received. If cumulative funding reaches ¥100 million during a fiscal year, a separate agreement is required before further commercial or business use, even if annual revenue is below the threshold.
